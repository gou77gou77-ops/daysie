Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daysie (Android package com.susuim.daysie, the "App"), published by susuim ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the App has no sign-up and no server of its own. What you enter stays on your phone. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

1. Information you enter stays on your device

The days you create (titles, dates, notes, emoji, colors, reminder settings), photos you attach, and your app settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your phone. We do not run any server and we never receive this information.

When you add a photo, the App uses Android's system photo picker, so it gets access only to the photo you choose. A resized copy is saved inside the App so it can appear on your cards and widgets.

2. Backup files

If you use "Export backup", the App creates a file containing your days and photos. You decide where to save or share it. We cannot access backup files.

3. Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and widgets are drawn on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language)
  • ad interactions (views and taps)

This information is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the App asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a day in the App removes it. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything the App stored. We hold no copy.
